# Approved product decisions

Recorded for Phase 0 on 2026-09-16.

- Private cause -> shared consequence. Partner assignments, feedback and raw Roots responses remain owner-only through database enforcement.
- Invite landing may reveal only the inviter's chosen first/display name as an intentional invitation disclosure. No other pre-acceptance profile or relationship data.
- Shared garden API/UI must not reveal which partner caused an event or the source assignment. Backend-only linkage is allowed.
- Shared Roots overlap is an intentional derived disclosure; complete raw answers remain private.
- One relationship timezone, established at creation.
- A partner joins the existing relationship at its current program day. No backfilled assignments for earlier days.
- Joining preserves relationship ID, path/start date, garden/history and each user's existing private data.
- After Day 28, the path may be completed; garden/history remains accessible. Detailed post-program UX is deferred.
- Roots product functionality is outside Milestone 1 except minimal schema/RLS/privacy testing.
- Begin with explicit garden refetch and focus/foreground refresh, without Realtime.
- Milestone 1 proves real authentication, invitation, private assignments, idempotent completion and shared garden persistence.
- No Expo work, production fixes or Phase 1 execution is authorized by Phase 0.
- Existing index.html and prototype assets remain untouched.
